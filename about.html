🎨 Hue How to Play 🧠 Brain Games

About

Hue is a daily color-match puzzle. One puzzle per day, the same color for everyone. You have 30 seconds and three sliders. Match the target as closely as you can, build a streak, and share your result.

How to Play

See the colored square on the left? That's the color you need to match! Mix red, green, and blue with the sliders to get as close as you can before the timer runs out. Tap Submit when you like your color, or just wait for time to end.

  • 🎯 Goal: match the target color in 30 seconds.
  • 🎚️ Sliders: drag R, G, and B to mix your color.
  • ✅ Submit: tap when you're happy with the match.
  • 🏆 Score: the closer your color, the higher your score!
