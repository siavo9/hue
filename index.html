Hue — the daily color-match puzzle

Hue is a free, browser-based daily puzzle game built around color perception. Every day there's a new target color, the same one for every player around the world. Your job is simple: mix red, green and blue to match it as closely as you can before the timer runs out.

How to Play

See the colored square on the left? That's the color you need to match! Mix red, green, and blue with the sliders to get as close as you can before the timer runs out. Tap Submit when you like your color, or just wait for time to end.

  • 🎯 Goal: match the target color in 30 seconds.
  • 🎚️ Sliders: drag R, G, and B to mix your color.
  • ✅ Submit: tap when you're happy with the match.
  • 🏆 Score: the closer your color, the higher your score!

Tips for a better score

  • Start by getting the overall brightness right, then nudge individual channels.
  • If the target looks warm, lean into red and green; if it looks cool, push blue.
  • Trust your eyes more than the numbers — small slider tweaks make big perceptual changes.
  • Come back every day to grow your streak. One puzzle per day, no ads, no sign-up.
